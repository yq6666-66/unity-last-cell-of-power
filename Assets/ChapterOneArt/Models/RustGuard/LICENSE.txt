RustGuard original procedural geometry

Created for LastCellOfPower, 2026-11-13.
The original geometry, Blender generator and generated model files in this directory
are made available under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The original design takes only a general abandoned-industrial atmosphere as its
reference. No third-party game character model, branding or artwork is included.
PBR texture sources used by the Unity material variants are documented separately
in the ChapterOneArt asset manifest and retain their own source/license records.

Source~/RustGuard.blend is the editable geometry source. The Source~ directory is
intentionally ignored by Unity to avoid an additional automatic Blender import.
RustGuard-preview.png is an offline Blender shape preview with plain materials,
not a screenshot of the Unity game or evidence of final PBR rendering.
